Notflix laboratory — Wikipedia reception extracts

The optional feature resolves a public IMDb identifier via Wikidata and selects
plain-text reception paragraphs from Wikipedia. It is not Rotten Tomatoes consensus,
a translation, an AI summary, or an endorsement by Wikimedia or the upstream projects.
The Kotlin implementation is original; no JavaScript is bundled or run.

Wikipedia text: Wikipedia contributors, Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The UI credits the particular article and permanent revision and identifies selection
and shortening. Copyright exceptions for third-party quotations may differ from the
article's general license; the conservative paragraph filter is not a universal guarantee.
Wikidata structured identity data is published under CC0.
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing

The feature uses the already declared jsoup dependency in the Full source set only.
No excerpt text, account cookies, user profile, library, progress, or viewing history is sent
to the providers. A public identifier and the required Wikipedia article title are sent.
